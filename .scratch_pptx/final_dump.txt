What is the Treasury Management System
www.solemitsolutions.com
A Digital Solution for Modern LGU Treasury Operations
A web-based platform designed specifically for Municipal and City Treasurer’s Offices to manage collections, disbursements, and accountable forms.
Enables secure multi-user collaboration with defined roles for Treasurers, Collectors, and Administrators.
Provides complete transparency and accountability through automated activity logs and audit trails.
Streamlines the entire revenue cycle—from collection and receipt issuance to bank deposit, reconciliation, and reporting.
Key capabilities include:
Payment capture across cash, cheque, online, and money order
Serial-number and batch validation for accountable forms
Bank deposit and cheque reconciliation
Real-time reporting and abstract generation

Project Objectives
www.solemitsolutions.com
What We Aim to Achieve
Streamline revenue collection – Simplify the recording of Official Receipts, Real Property Tax, Cedulas, Marriage Certificates, and Burial Permits.
Enhance collaboration – Enable multiple collectors and treasury staff to work simultaneously with role-based access.

Enable quick retrieval – Provide search and filtering across collection logs, report logs, and activity records.

Project Scope and Delimitations
www.solemitsolutions.com
What’s included and What’s Not (Initial Phase)
Included in the Initial Phase:

System Modules and Features
www.solemitsolutions.com
Core System Modules
Built for Efficiency and Ease of Use
| Module | Key Features |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Centralized overview with collection statistics, pending cancel requests, recent activity, and notification summaries. |
| Collection Management | Record transactions across 6 form types–Official Receipt, OR/RPT, Individual & Corporation Cedula, Marriage Certificate, and Burial Permit–capturing payment method and amount per transaction. |
| Official Receipts & Accountable Forms | Add serial-numbered form batches, validate against duplicate or already-used serials, and track issued vs. remaining stock per form. |
| User Management | Role-based access control—assign permissions per user role (Admin, Treasurer, Collector, Staff). |
| Banks Deposit & Reconciliation | Record bank deposits from pending cash, cheque, and money-order collections; confirm online payments; mark cheques cleared or bounced. |
| Cheque Management | Issue and track disbursement cheques, cancel or reissue, and generate the Report of Checks Issued. |
| Reporting & Abstract | Generate the Treasurer’s Monthly Report of Accountability, CRAAF, and Summary of Collections in styled, exportable formats. |
| Records | System-wide activity log searchable and filterable by module, with a separate Archive for completed or cancelled records. |

System Security and Risk Management
www.solemitsolutions.com
Protecting Your Data and Ensuring Compliance
To maintain data integrity, confidentiality, and availability, the system will implement:
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) – Users only access features and data relevant to their role.
Secure User Authentication – Strong password policies and optional multi-factor authentication.
Activity Logging and Audit Trails – Every action is logged with user ID, timestamp, and IP address.
Encrypted Database Connections – All data transmitted between the server and users is encrypted using SSL/TLS.
Regular System Backups – Automated daily/weekly backups to prevent data loss.
Restricted Administrator Access – Only authorized personnel have administrative privileges.
Risk Mitigation:
Regular security assessments and vulnerability scanning.
Incident response plan in case of system issues.
